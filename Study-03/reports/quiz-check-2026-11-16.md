## Details and sources

### hist-11: Which ship carried the Pilgrims from England to North America in 1620? (Mayflower)
- Answer: confirmed. The Mayflower left Plymouth, England, in September 1620 and anchored off Cape Cod in November 1620 before the settlers moved to the Plymouth site.
- Wrong options: Santa Maria (Columbus, 1492), Endeavour (Cook), Beagle (Darwin): all clearly wrong.
- Explanation: accurate.
- Sources: [Britannica: Mayflower](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Mayflower-ship), [History.com: Mayflower](https://www.history.com/topics/colonial-america/mayflower)

### hist-12: Who crowned himself Emperor of the French at Notre-Dame in Paris in 1804? (Napoleon Bonaparte)
- Answer: confirmed. Crowned on 2 December 1804 in Notre-Dame, with Pope Pius VII present; Napoleon placed the crown on his own head.
- Note: the more dramatic story of him snatching the crown from the pope is legend; the question and explanation only state that he crowned himself, which is well documented.
- Wrong options: Louis XIV, Charles de Gaulle, Cardinal Richelieu: none was ever Emperor of the French.
- Sources: [Britannica: The Coronation of Napoleon](https://www.britannica.com/topic/The-Coronation-of-Napoleon), [Fondation Napoléon](https://www.napoleon.org/en/history-of-the-two-empires/close-up/a-close-up-on-the-coronation-of-napoleon-i), [World History Encyclopedia](https://www.worldhistory.org/article/2251/coronation-of-napoleon-i/)

### hist-13: Which Roman emperor dedicated Constantinople, the former Byzantium, as his new capital in 330 CE? (Constantine the Great)
- Answer: confirmed. Dedicated on 11 May 330 on the site of Byzantium.
- Wrong options: Augustus (first emperor, ruled from Rome), Justinian I (later Byzantine emperor), Hadrian: all wrong.
- Sources: [Britannica: Constantine I](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Constantine-I-Roman-emperor), [World History Encyclopedia: Constantinople](https://www.worldhistory.org/Constantinople/), [Oxford University Press blog](https://blog.oup.com/2012/05/constantine-dedicates-constantinople/)

### sci-11: At sea level, at what temperature in degrees Celsius does pure water boil? (100)
- Answer: confirmed, with the conditions the guidelines ask for (pure water, sea level, Celsius). The exact value at 1 atm is 99.97 °C; 100 °C is the standard figure, and no other option is close, so there is only one correct answer.
- Wrong options: 90, 120, and 212 (the Fahrenheit value, a fair distractor since the question says Celsius).
- Sources: [Britannica: boiling point](https://britannica.com/science/boiling-point), [New World Encyclopedia: boiling point](https://www.newworldencyclopedia.org/entry/Boiling_point)

### sci-12: Which organ in the human body produces the hormone insulin? (Pancreas)
- Answer: confirmed. Insulin is made only by beta cells in the pancreatic islets of Langerhans.
- Wrong options: liver, kidney, spleen: none produces insulin.
- Sources: [NIDDK Diabetes Branch (archived)](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/eot2008/20090130201135/http://www2.niddk.nih.gov/NIDDKLabs/AllLabs/Branches/DiabetesBranch.htm), [NIH news release on islet cells](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/news-releases/nih-funded-study-maps-human-pancreatic-islet-cells-offering-new-clues-diabetes-risk)

### sci-13: Which mineral is rated 10, the highest hardness, on the Mohs scale? (Diamond)
- Answer: confirmed; the explanation's values (corundum 9, topaz 8, quartz 7) are also correct. The measure is stated ("on the Mohs scale"), as guideline 2 asks.
- Sources: [California State University Northridge: Mohs hardness](https://www.csun.edu/science/geoscience/geology/mineral/mohs_hardness.html), [International Gem Society: Mohs hardness chart](https://www.gemsociety.org/article/hardness-chart/)

### geo-11: On which continent is the Sahara Desert? (Africa)
- Answer and explanation confirmed: bordered by the Atlantic to the west and the Red Sea to the east, across North Africa.
- Sources: [Britannica: Sahara](https://www.britannica.com/place/Sahara-desert-Africa), [Live Science: Sahara](https://www.livescience.com/23140-sahara-desert.html)

### geo-12: Which country is the largest in the world by total area? (Russia)
- Answer confirmed, and the measure is stated ("by total area"). Russia about 17.1 million km² (Britannica 17,075,400 km²; CIA 17,098,242 km²); Canada second at about 9.98 million km².
- Wrong options: Canada, China, United States: all smaller.
- Sources: [Britannica: largest countries by area](https://britannica.com/topic/list-of-the-total-areas-of-the-worlds-countries-dependencies-and-territories-2130540), [WorldAtlas: largest countries](https://www.worldatlas.com/geography/the-largest-countries-in-the-world.html)

### geo-13: Which strait separates Spain in Europe from Morocco in Africa? (Strait of Gibraltar)
- Answer and explanation confirmed. About 13 km wide at its narrowest.
- Wrong options: Bosporus (Turkey), Strait of Hormuz (Persian Gulf), Strait of Messina (Italy/Sicily): all wrong.
- Sources: [Britannica: Strait of Gibraltar](https://www.britannica.com/place/Strait-of-Gibraltar), [NASA: the Strait of Gibraltar](https://www.nasa.gov/image-feature/the-strait-of-gibraltar-connects-the-atlantic-to-the-mediterranean)

### art-11: Which instrument has 88 keys in its standard modern form? (Piano)
- Answer and explanation confirmed: 88 keys, 52 white and 36 black, standard since about the 1890s.
- Wrong options: organ (manuals are usually 61 keys), harpsichord (usually fewer), accordion: all wrong.
- Sources: [Classic FM: why pianos have 88 keys](https://classicfm.com/discover-music/instruments/piano/why-pianos-have-88-keys/), [Merriam Music: how many keys on a piano](https://www.merriammusic.com/pianos/how-many-piano-keys-strings/)
- Note: these are music-industry sources rather than encyclopedias; they agree with each other and with the question's own `sourceHint` (Britannica: piano).

### art-12: Which author wrote the novel Nineteen Eighty-Four? (George Orwell)
- Answer and explanation confirmed: published 8 June 1949 by Secker & Warburg.
- Wrong options: Aldous Huxley (Brave New World), Ray Bradbury (Fahrenheit 451), H. G. Wells: plausible dystopia authors, all wrong.
- Sources: [Britannica: Nineteen Eighty-four](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Nineteen-Eighty-four), [History Today](https://www.historytoday.com/archive/months-past/publication-george-orwell%E2%80%99s-nineteen-eighty-four), [The Open University](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/society-politics-law/george-orwell-and-nineteen-eighty-four/content-section-4)

### art-13: Which ancient Greek playwright wrote the tragedy Oedipus Rex? (Sophocles)
- Answer confirmed. First performed in Athens around 429 BCE; the explanation's "5th century BCE" is accurate.
- Wrong options: Euripides and Aeschylus (fellow tragedians), Aristophanes (comedies): all wrong.
- Sources: [Encyclopedia.com: Oedipus Rex](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/culture-magazines/oedipus-rex), [Toronto Metropolitan University Pressbooks: Oedipus Rex](https://pressbooks.library.torontomu.ca/oedipusrex/?p=10)
