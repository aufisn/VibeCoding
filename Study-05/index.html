      Delete the whole journal

      This removes every entry from your Journal for all your devices. Older versions remain in your Gist's revision history on GitHub. Don't delete the Gist itself; FridgeChef uses it too. This also removes month files that can't be opened (damaged, or locked with a different passphrase).

      Privacy: Your Journal is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your passphrase) in your secret Gist, so your phone and computer share it. Nothing of your diary is kept in this browser. The Gist's file names show which months have entries, but never what's in them. If you lose your passphrase, the Journal can't be read and there is no way to recover it.
